Insightful, formerly Workpuls, is an employee monitoring and workforce analytics tool that helps managers see how their team spends time on their computers. The desktop agent logs work hours, apps, websites, and idle time, and it even offers an option to capture screenshots at set intervals. Plans start at $10 per seat per month.
